Use 10 more letters?

This adds b f g k l n p s w z, so far more words fit. But fewer fonts include these letters, so on many phones, apps and computers they show up as empty boxes or in the wrong spot.

If yours look wrong, that's how they'll look here. Even if they look right, they can still break for people you send them to. j, q and y have no small form at all.
